## 🎯 Goals

1. Buy a reliable family car (MPV or SUV) under IDR 150 juta
2. Pay by installment (kredit) — monthly payment under IDR 3 juta
3. Find a car with a clean legal document (STNK + BPKB valid)
4. Complete the transaction safely — avoid fraud
5. Get a car that can handle Jakarta traffic and occasional out-of-city trips

---

## 😣 Frustrations

1. **Cannot verify car condition remotely** — photos on listings do not show real problems
2. **Odometer and flood damage fraud** — bad actors manipulate mileage or hide flood history
3. **Price is not transparent** — same car has very different prices on different platforms
4. **Slow financing process** — bank loan approval takes days; he needs a fast answer
5. **Sellers disappear or change price** when he contacts them on WhatsApp
6. **Fear of buying a "lemon"** — he does not have technical car knowledge

---

## 🛒 Behavior

### Discovery Phase (1–3 weeks)
- Searches OLX, Garasi.id, and Mobil88 on mobile phone
- Compares prices across 3–5 platforms before contacting any seller
- Asks family and coworkers for recommendations
- Watches YouTube reviews for specific car models (Toyota Avanza, Daihatsu Xenia, Mitsubishi Xpander)

### Evaluation Phase (1–2 weeks)
- Shortlists 3–4 cars
- Calls or WhatsApps sellers
- Asks friend or mechanic to inspect car before buying
- Checks document legality manually (STNK, BPKB, pajak)

### Decision Phase
- Buys from dealer (not private seller) if budget allows — more trust
- Prefers certified pre-owned programs (Toyota Trust, Mobil88) for peace of mind
- Uses bank financing (BCA, BRI, Mandiri) or dealer financing

## ⚡ Needs from a Platform

### High Priority
- **Vehicle inspection report** — clear, easy to read, trustworthy
- **Price comparison tool** — show similar cars and their prices
- **Financing calculator** — instant DP and installment estimate
- **Seller verification badge** — shows seller is a registered dealer or verified individual

### Medium Priority
- Saved search and price alert
- WhatsApp integration for fast contact
- Review of sellers by previous buyers

### Low Priority
- Virtual showroom / 360° view
- EV / hybrid options
- Auction features

---

## 🧠 Mental Model

Budi does not trust the car. He trusts the process.

He wants steps, not just information. He wants to know:
1. Is this car safe to buy?
2. Can I afford the monthly payment?
3. What do I do if something goes wrong?

A platform that answers these 3 questions clearly will win his trust.

---

## ❌ Anti-Patterns (What Makes Him Leave a Platform)

- Too many listings with no inspection data
- Prices listed "nego" (negotiable) without any anchor price
- Slow-loading pages or complex navigation on mobile
- No clear contact button — must find seller info manually
- Platform pushes him to buy new car instead of used
